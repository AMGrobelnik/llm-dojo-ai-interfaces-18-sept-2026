# credibility.md — per-source scrutiny pass (the criteria the SIFT ledger read applies)

**Budget: ~1–2 fetches per source, spent OFF the source.** Experts judge a site by leaving it ["fact checkers read laterally"](https://stacks.stanford.edu/file/druid:yk133ht8603/Wineburg%20McGrew_Lateral%20Reading%20and%20the%20Nature%20of%20Expertise.pdf).
**Directionality:** only OFF-site evidence can promote; on-page evidence only DOWNGRADES. Credible negative reputation forces red — never averaged against positives [raters](https://guidelines.raterhub.com/searchqualityevaluatorguidelines.pdf).

## Core pass — mandatory for every recurring source/author/venue, memoized

1. **[script] Ledger + RSP lookup (free):** a Wikipedia perennial-sources entry inherits tier + scope notes; absence from any list is zero signal [WP:RSP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Reliable_sources/Perennial_sources).
2. **[per-source] Lateral reputation search (fetch 1):** `"<source>" (fact check OR reliability OR misinformation) -site:<domain>`, independent hits only. Track record carries the heaviest weight in both major scorers (NewsGuard 22/100, MBFC 40%): repeated UNCORRECTED false content on important topics = red [NewsGuard](https://www.newsguardtech.com/ratings/rating-process-criteria/), [MBFC](https://mediabiasfactcheck.com/methodology/). Judge corrections behavior OFF-site in these same results — did the outlet correct when a third party caught it? Corrected errors don't count against: ["Only uncorrected errors negatively impact the score"](https://mediabiasfactcheck.com/methodology/). Also weigh `shall`/`should` language — pre-calibrated constraint strength.
3. **[per-source] Fetch the best independent hit (fetch 2):** this page supplies the ledger's ONE verbatim ["quote"](https://example.com) — search snippets can't pass `verify_quotes.py`. Adoption-by-reference (already-green outlets citing it for facts without caveat) is the hardest-to-buy positive signal in our survey [WP:UBO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Reliable_sources).

## Structural check — exactly ONE, routed by source type; runs only if the core pass leaves the verdict undecided (re-spends the fetch-2 slot)

- **Journal** → one editorial-board member's OWN page acknowledging the journal — person-exists-in-field is NOT verification (predatory journals list real researchers without consent); unverifiable boards were the top predatory discriminator, 73% vs 2%, and the check survives 2026 because the deciding evidence is off-site [Shamseer 2017](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12916-017-0785-9). [script] DOAJ lookup (beware indexes that list without vetting). Guaranteed acceptance or days-long review = red; bogus metrics (Index Copernicus Value: 33% predatory vs 3% legitimate) = strong downgrade confirmed via the board check, never auto-red [Think.Check.Submit](https://thinkchecksubmit.org/journals/).
- **News outlet** → deepen the corrections/track-record read of step 2; a `site:<domain> correction` search shows the policy is implemented [W3C signals](https://credweb.org/reviewed-signals-20200224/) — demote-only when absent, zero as a positive (a dated corrections archive is fabricatable). If still undecided, read ONE sampled article: headline matches body; claims attributed to identifiable, preferably firsthand sources (2+ named unless one is uniquely authoritative); links resolve — ALL demote-only; passing is zero signal, 2026 farm output derives headlines from bodies and ships real resolving links [NewsGuard](https://www.newsguardtech.com/ratings/rating-process-criteria/), [IFCN](https://ifcncodeofprinciples.poynter.org/the-commitments).
- **Org / vendor** → ownership: fetch the about page; the fetch-1 results must corroborate the named owner. A concealed operator caps the verdict at orange even when every other check passes [Breakstone](https://kappanonline.org/breakstone-need-new-approach-teaching-digital-literacy/), [NewsGuard](https://www.newsguardtech.com/ratings/rating-process-criteria/). A stake in the claim (own product, funder interest, advocacy goal) moves those claims out of the citation lane [WP:RS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Reliable_sources).
- **Blog / individual author** → byline verifies OFF-site via a body of work beyond this site ["good professional reputation as a journalist"](https://thetrustproject.org/trust-indicators/); on-site bio + headshot + author markup = zero — farms attribute AI content to in-house writers [NewsGuard](https://www.newsguardtech.com/ratings/rating-process-criteria/). Self-claimed credentials and displayed badges count only after confirmation at the claimed institution or the ISSUER's registry [raters](https://guidelines.raterhub.com/searchqualityevaluatorguidelines.pdf), [IFCN](https://ifcncodeofprinciples.poynter.org/the-commitments).

## Conditional checks — run only when a triage tell or the routed check flags

- **[per-source] AI-origin / reference integrity** — the most paraphrase-robust signal in our survey: resolve sampled DOIs/ISBNs AND compare returned metadata to the citation text; a valid DOI on the wrong paper is the fabrication fingerprint [WP:AI-signs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing). Chatbot artifacts (site-search "as an AI language model" / cross-article template repetition): a hit under a human byline = fail; a CLEAN search is zero signal — competent farms strip error strings [NewsGuard](https://www.newsguardtech.com/ratings/rating-process-criteria/). A farm verdict needs ALL FOUR: substantial AI content + no human oversight + human-seeming presentation + no disclosure; disclosed, human-edited AI use is NOT disqualifying [NewsGuard UAIN](https://www.newsguardtech.com/special-reports/ai-tracking-center).
- **[per-source] Wayback continuity** (only when age is load-bearing): earliest snapshot PLUS one old snapshot showing the same publisher/topic; failure = age counts zero (expired-domain reuse); a pass = weak positive only, never green-deciding — aged domains with topical continuity are purchasable [W3C signals](https://credweb.org/reviewed-signals-20200224/), [raters](https://guidelines.raterhub.com/searchqualityevaluatorguidelines.pdf).
- **[per-source] Funder disclosure** (fact-check/nonprofit orgs only — IFCN signatories name each funder ≥5% of revenue): check that a funder table exists at all; demote-only, a self-published table never promotes [IFCN](https://ifcncodeofprinciples.poynter.org/the-commitments).
- **[per-source] Opinion/advocacy labeling:** disclosed slant scopes reliable-for ("facts yes, framing no"), never demotes alone [NewsGuard](https://www.newsguardtech.com/ratings/rating-process-criteria/). Preprints citable only with an explicit not-peer-reviewed scope [Think.Check.Submit](https://thinkchecksubmit.org/journals/). A lone preprint never anchors a settled-canon / gap row — re-anchor to the newest credible synthesis or the defining work (dead-end and assumption-challenge rows exempt: the defining retrospective / negative result / named dismissal / critique IS the anchor — a lone-preprint challenge half cites with an explicit not-peer-reviewed scope; §Verdict currency still applies).
- **[per-page] Sponsored/advertorial scan:** sponsored content never inherits its domain's green — it is designed to look like the surrounding editorial [WP:RSP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Reliable_sources/Perennial_sources).
- **[per-source] PubPeer:** a cited paper with a substantive thread (their bar: publicly verifiable evidence) = vetted concern → orange, not red [PubPeer](https://pubpeer.com/static/faq).

## Per-claim moves — separate budget, spent on the CLAIM in the quote-verification flow, never memoized as a source verdict

- Trace one citation hop: fetch a cited original, confirm same meaning; a source that misstates its own citations demotes regardless of all else [SIFT](https://hapgood.us/2019/06/19/sift-the-four-moves/).
- Find better coverage: one search — if a higher-tier source carries the claim, cite that; the original drops to mining-lane, as does secondary-only sourcing where primaries exist [SIFT](https://hapgood.us/2019/06/19/sift-the-four-moves/), [IFCN](https://ifcncodeofprinciples.poynter.org/the-commitments).
- **Ceiling-claim grounding targets:** *what's-been-done / settled-canon* → quote the defining work or the survey naming it; *assumption row* → TWO quotes: the assumption from the canon/survey that RELIES on it AND the challenge from the critique / replication failure / named dissent (lone preprint acceptable for the challenge half, scoped not-peer-reviewed); *important-problem* → quote the importance assertion AND the dated enabler (why-tractable-NOW); *dead-end / not-worth-it* → quote the paper or named expert saying so (retrospective, negative result, position paper) — never inference from absent results; *novelty-taste / eval-norm* → quote an award/meta-review rationale, methods paper, or venue guideline; *opening* → inherits its parent map rows' anchors. Same entailment bar: the span states the map/gap, not just the topic.
- **[script]** DOI resolution + metadata match; retraction gate (Retraction Watch data is open via Crossref): retraction = red for that paper, expression-of-concern = orange [Crossref](https://www.crossref.org/blog/news-crossref-and-retraction-watch/).

## Verdict rubric — every source starts orange

- **green → citation-lane.** Verbatim ["quote"](https://example.com) anchors allowed, inside the reliable-for scope. Requires positive INDEPENDENT (off-site) evidence — track record or adoption-by-reference — never one lucky page. Honest floor: our 1–2-page sample is far below MBFC's minimum (10 headlines + 5 full stories) [MBFC](https://mediabiasfactcheck.com/methodology/); a green here is a working verdict, weaker than a rater's, re-earned when the source becomes load-bearing.
- **orange → mining-lane only.** Leads, terminology, citations to chase; every shipped claim re-anchored to a green source. The default state, and the ceiling for concealed ownership.
- **red → no citations, no third-party claims;** usable for about-self statements only. A ledger-confirmed **deny** flag (set only after investigation; Wikipedia's deprecated semantics) goes further: a claim found ONLY there is presumed incorrect, not merely unverified ["it may be valid to assume that the material in question is incorrect"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Deprecated_sources). External deny-list membership is a LEAD that routes a source here for investigation, never itself a verdict; the deny gate keys on ledger-confirmed deny entries only (agent-run — see §Ledger integration).

Reliable-for scoping is mandatory on green AND orange ("green for its own API behavior, orange for benchmark comparisons") — authority is domain-relative, and unlikely voices can be authoritative in-niche [ACRL](https://www.ala.org/acrl/standards/ilframework). Claim certainty is a separate axis: attach the coarse evidence-type tier (synthesis > primary study > anecdote) to the CLAIM's scope note, never to the source verdict [GRADE](https://gdt.gradepro.org/app/handbook/handbook.html).

## Verdict currency

- Date every verdict: behavioral verdicts decay (~4y); structural ones (self-published, user-generated) don't; ownership/editorial change = era boundary, never retroactive [WP:RSP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Reliable_sources/Perennial_sources).
- Synthesis/review older than ~5y in an active field: presume superseded — scope "as of <year>", one search for a newer one [WP:MEDRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Identifying_reliable_sources_(medicine)).

## Ledger integration

This file IS the criteria list the memoized SIFT lateral read applies: one scrutiny per recurring source/author/venue, reused for every later quote from it. Ledger entry = verdict + reliable-for scope + the deciding check + ONE verbatim ["quote"](https://example.com) of the strongest independent evidence — re-verified by the same `scripts/verify_quotes.py` pass as handbook claims.
`scripts/check_sources.py` enforces the structural half only: [Sn]↔row resolution, row completeness (tier · scope · quote), shared-origin grouping, preprint flags, candidate-lane hygiene. The `[script]`-tagged moves above (RSP lookup, DOAJ lookup, DOI resolution + metadata match, retraction gate, deny gate) are mechanical but NOT yet scripted — run them per source/claim by hand; a clean verifier exit does not mean they ran.

## Do not weight (defeated or cosmetic, 2026)

Polish/fluency/typo-free prose · calm neutral tone · official-looking logos · domain names/TLDs · on-site bylines+bios · badge pixels · mission statements · policy pages without a behavior trail · on-site corrections archives as a positive (fabricatable at near-zero cost; demote-only when absent on a news source) · headline–body consistency as a positive · presence of working outbound links · self-published funder tables · absence of chatbot artifacts · traffic/domain-authority/SEO ranks · follower counts and platform votes · citation counts (tie-break at best) · raw domain age · ClaimReview markup (Google stopped surfacing it, 2025) · C2PA absence (platforms strip manifests) · WHOIS identity (redacted since 2025) · AI-detector scores and style tells (triage only — a fired tell routes to the reference-integrity check, never to a verdict) · external deny-list membership (a lead to investigate, not a verdict).

## Scope note

These criteria make the judgment AUDITABLE — every verdict names the check and fetched evidence that decided it — not objective. The checklist-theater critique applies to this checklist too ["checklists may lead students astray"](https://kappanonline.org/breakstone-need-new-approach-teaching-digital-literacy/); the defenses: every check that can PROMOTE requires off-site evidence the source's operator does not control, on-site checks only demote, one failed lateral check outweighs any number of passed cosmetic ones, and the mandatory core fits the 2-fetch budget — nothing must be pencil-whipped from priors. Rubric authors document their own misfires (NewsGuard concedes its byline criterion penalizes outlets where naming journalists is unsafe); expect these criteria to misfire too — scope the verdict, don't defend it. Classify venue/type/recency and STOP: no re-refereeing a higher-tier synthesis from the agent's own priors [WP:MEDRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Identifying_reliable_sources_(medicine)).
