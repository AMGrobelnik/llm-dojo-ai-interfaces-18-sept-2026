## What one board is holding up

Every group refitted with one board deleted, once per board, and the
worst move any of the top 10 rows makes when it goes, and how many of
the top 5 it swaps out. The fit is least squares and least squares
does not report how much of its answer rests on any single input, so
this asks the question the only way sparse evidence allows: take the
board away and see what happens. A big move is not an error — it is
the group telling you which board it is actually ranking on, which is
worth knowing before trusting the order. Rows that fall under 3
boards once the board is gone leave the comparison rather than
counting as a move.
